# Sales Performance Dashboard

## **Project Overview**
This Excel dashboard analyzes sales performance for a fictional retail business. It tracks **sales, quantity, and profit** by region and product, provides monthly trend insights, and highlights key metrics through interactive charts and KPIs.  

The goal of this project is to demonstrate **Excel skills in data aggregation, visualization, and interactivity**, suitable for a data-related role.

---

## **Dataset**
- Columns included:  
  - `OrderID` – Unique identifier for each transaction  
  - `Date` – Transaction date  
  - `Region` – East, West, South, North  
  - `Product` – Product sold  
  - `Sales` – Transaction amount (USD)  
  - `Quantity` – Number of units sold  
  - `Profit` – Profit from each transaction (Sales − Cost)  

> The dataset contains 50 rows of sample data created for portfolio purposes.

---

## **Key Skills & Concepts Demonstrated**
- **Formulas:** `SUMIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`, `IF`  
- **PivotTables:** Aggregate sales and profit by region, product, and month  
- **PivotCharts:** Visual representation of top products and sales trends  
- **Conditional Formatting:** Highlight high/low sales and profits  
- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity  
- **Slicers & Filters:** Interactive dashboard filtering by Region and Product  

---

## **Dashboard Features**
1. **Sales by Region:** Column chart showing total sales and profit per region  
2. **Monthly Sales Trend:** Line chart showing month-over-month sales  
3. **Top 5 Products:** Column chart of top-selling products  
4. **KPI Cards:** Quick view of Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Quantity  
5. **Conditional Formatting:** Highlights significant data points automatically  
6. **Interactive Slicers:** Filter the dashboard dynamically by Region and Product  

---

## **How to Use**
1. Download `Sales_Performance_Dashboard.xlsx`.  
2. Open in **Microsoft Excel (2016 or later)**.  
3. Use slicers to filter data and explore interactive charts.  
4. Review KPI cards for a quick summary of key metrics.
