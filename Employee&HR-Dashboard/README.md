# Employee / HR Dashboard

## **Project Overview**
This Excel dashboard analyzes employee data for a fictional organization. It tracks **headcount, salary, department distribution, and performance ratings**, providing interactive insights into workforce metrics.  

The goal of this project is to demonstrate **Excel skills in data aggregation, visualization, and interactivity**, useful for HR analytics or general data analysis roles.

---

## **Dataset**
- Columns included:  
  - `EmployeeID` – Unique identifier for each employee  
  - `Name` – Employee name  
  - `Department` – Department name (e.g., IT, Marketing, HR)  
  - `Gender` – Male/Female/Other  
  - `Salary` – Monthly or yearly salary (USD)  
  - `PerformanceRating` – Score or rating (1–5)  

> The dataset contains 50 sample rows created for portfolio purposes.

---

## **Key Skills & Concepts Demonstrated**
- **Formulas:** `COUNTIF`, `COUNTIFS`, `AVERAGEIF`  
- **PivotTables:** Aggregate employee counts, salaries, and performance by department or gender  
- **PivotCharts:** Visualize department distribution, salary breakdown, and performance trends  
- **Conditional Formatting:** Highlight high/low salaries or performance ratings  
- **KPI Cards:** Total Employees, Average Salary, Average Performance  
- **Slicers & Filters:** Filter data dynamically by department or gender  

---

## **Dashboard Features**
1. **Employee Count by Department:** Column chart showing number of employees per department  
2. **Gender Distribution:** Pie chart representing male/female/other distribution  
3. **Salary Overview:** PivotChart showing average and total salary per department  
4. **Performance Ratings:** Bar chart or heatmap showing employee performance  
5. **KPI Cards:** Quick view of total employees, average salary, and average performance rating  
6. **Interactive Slicers:** Filter dashboard by Department or Gender  

---

## **How to Use**
1. Download `Employee_HR_Dashboard.xlsx`.  
2. Open in **Microsoft Excel (2016 or later)**.  
3. Use slicers to filter by department or gender for dynamic insights.  
4. Review KPI cards for a quick summary of workforce metrics.  

---

## **Conclusion**
This dashboard demonstrates practical **Excel skills** for workforce analysis and HR reporting. It’s a **ready-to-show portfolio project** for freshers aiming for **data analyst or business intelligence roles**.
