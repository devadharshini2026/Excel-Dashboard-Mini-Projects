# Financial Dashboard

## **Project Overview**
This Excel dashboard tracks the financial performance of a fictional company. It analyzes **revenue, expenses, profit, and budget vs actual** to provide insights into financial health and trends.  

The goal of this project is to demonstrate **Excel skills in financial analysis, visualization, and interactive reporting**, suitable for roles in finance, analytics, or business intelligence.

---

## **Dataset**
- Columns included:  
  - `Month` – Month of the financial data (e.g., Jan-2025, Feb-2025)  
  - `Revenue` – Income generated in that month  
  - `Expenses` – Total expenses for the month  
  - `Profit` – Calculated as `Revenue − Expenses`  
  - `Budget` – Planned budget for comparison  

> The dataset contains 12 months of sample financial data created for portfolio purposes.

---

## **Key Skills & Concepts Demonstrated**
- **Formulas:** Profit (`=Revenue−Expenses`), Budget Variance (`=Revenue−Budget`)  
- **PivotTables:** Aggregate financial data by month, quarter, or year  
- **PivotCharts:** Column/Line charts showing revenue, expenses, and profit trends  
- **Conditional Formatting:** Highlight high/low profit or budget variances  
- **KPI Cards:** Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Total Profit, Budget Achievement  
- **Slicers & Filters:** Interactive filtering by month or quarter  

---

## **Dashboard Features**
1. **Revenue vs Expenses:** Column chart showing monthly revenue and expenses  
2. **Profit Trend:** Line chart showing profit over months  
3. **Budget vs Actual:** Column chart showing planned vs actual revenue  
4. **KPI Cards:** Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Total Profit, Budget Achievement  
5. **Conditional Formatting:** Highlights months with high/low profit or overspent budget  
6. **Interactive Slicers:** Filter dashboard by month or quarter  

---

## **How to Use**
1. Download `Financial_Dashboard.xlsx`.  
2. Open in **Microsoft Excel (2016 or later)**.  
3. Use slicers to filter months or quarters dynamically.  
4. Explore PivotTables, charts, and KPI cards for financial insights.  

---

## **Conclusion**
This dashboard demonstrates practical **Excel skills** for financial analysis and reporting. It’s a **ready-to-show portfolio project** for freshers aiming for **data analyst, finance analyst, or business intelligence roles**.
