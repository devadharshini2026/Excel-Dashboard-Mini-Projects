# Customer Analytics Dashboard

## **Project Overview**
This Excel dashboard analyzes customer behavior and purchasing patterns. It tracks **orders, total spending, frequency, and top customers**, providing actionable insights for business decision-making.  

The goal of this project is to demonstrate **Excel skills in customer analytics, data aggregation, visualization, and interactive reporting**, suitable for data analyst roles.

---

## **Dataset**
- Columns included:  
  - `CustomerID` – Unique identifier for each customer  
  - `Name` – Customer name  
  - `Orders` – Number of orders placed  
  - `TotalSpent` – Total amount spent (USD)  
  - `Region` – Customer location  

> The dataset contains 50 sample rows created for portfolio purposes.

---

## **Key Skills & Concepts Demonstrated**
- **Formulas:** SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIFS  
- **PivotTables:** Aggregate metrics by customer, region, or product  
- **PivotCharts:** Bar chart for top customers, Pie chart for regional distribution  
- **Conditional Formatting:** Highlight high/low spenders or frequent customers  
- **KPI Cards:** Total Customers, Total Orders, Total Revenue, Average Order Value  
- **Slicers & Filters:** Interactive filtering by Region or Customer segment  

---

## **Dashboard Features**
1. **Top Customers by Revenue:** Column chart showing highest spending customers  
2. **Customer Orders Distribution:** Pie chart representing order distribution by region  
3. **Customer Spending Trend:** Line chart showing revenue per customer or segment  
4. **KPI Cards:** Total Customers, Total Orders, Total Revenue, Average Order Value  
5. **Conditional Formatting:** Highlights high-value customers or frequent buyers  
6. **Interactive Slicers:** Filter dashboard by Region or Customer segment  

---

## **How to Use**
1. Download `Customer_Analytics_Dashboard.xlsx`.  
2. Open in **Microsoft Excel (2016 or later)**.  
3. Use slicers to filter by region or customer segment dynamically.  
4. Explore PivotTables, charts, and KPI cards for actionable insights.

## **Conclusion**
This dashboard demonstrates practical **Excel skills** for customer analytics and reporting. It’s a **ready-to-show portfolio project** for freshers aiming for **data analyst, business intelligence, or marketing analyst roles**.
