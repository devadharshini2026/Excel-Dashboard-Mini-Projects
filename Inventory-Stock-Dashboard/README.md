# Inventory / Stock Dashboard

## **Project Overview**
This Excel dashboard monitors inventory levels, product sales, and reorder alerts for a fictional retail business. It provides insights into **stock availability, sales trends, and low-stock items**, helping in inventory management decisions.  

The goal of this project is to demonstrate **Excel skills in data aggregation, visualization, and interactive reporting**, suitable for data analyst or supply chain roles.

---

## **Dataset**
- Sample dataset contains 50 products with the following columns:  
  - `ItemID` – Unique identifier for each product  
  - `ItemName` – Product name  
  - `Category` – Product category (Electronics, Furniture, Stationery)  
  - `StockLevel` – Current stock quantity  
  - `ReorderLevel` – Minimum stock level before reorder is required  
  - `Sales` – Units sold  

> Dataset is included in `Inventory_Stock_Dashboard.xlsx` and can also be used for practice to build interactive dashboards.

---

## **Key Skills & Concepts Demonstrated**
- **Formulas:** IF/AND for low-stock alerts, SUMIFS for sales aggregation  
- **PivotTables:** Aggregate stock, sales, and reorder alerts by category or product  
- **PivotCharts:** Column/Bar charts for stock levels and sales trends  
- **Conditional Formatting:** Highlight low-stock items or overstocked products  
- **KPI Cards:** Total Items, Total Stock, Total Sales, Low-Stock Alerts  
- **Slicers & Filters:** Interactive filtering by Category or Product  

---

## **Dashboard Features**
1. **Stock Levels by Product:** Column chart showing current stock per item  
2. **Sales Trends by Category:** Bar chart displaying sales per category  
3. **Low-Stock Alerts:** Conditional formatting to highlight items below reorder level  
4. **KPI Cards:** Total Items, Total Stock, Total Sales, Low-Stock Alerts  
5. **Interactive Slicers:** Filter dashboard by Product Category  
6. **Conditional Formatting:** Automatically flags critical stock levels  

---

## **How to Use**
1. Download `Inventory_Stock_Dashboard.xlsx`.  
2. Open in **Microsoft Excel (2016 or later)**.  
3. Explore PivotTables, charts, KPI cards, and conditional formatting.  
4. Use slicers to filter by category or product dynamically.
