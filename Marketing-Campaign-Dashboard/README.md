# Marketing Campaign Dashboard

## **Project Overview**
This Excel dashboard analyzes the performance of marketing campaigns. It tracks **impressions, clicks, conversions, and ROI** across channels, providing insights into campaign effectiveness.  

The goal of this project is to demonstrate **Excel skills in data aggregation, visualization, and interactive reporting**, useful for digital marketing analytics or general data analysis roles.

---

## **Dataset**
- Columns included:  
  - `CampaignID` – Unique identifier for each marketing campaign  
  - `Channel` – Marketing channel (e.g., Email, Social Media, PPC)  
  - `Impressions` – Number of times the campaign was shown  
  - `Clicks` – Number of clicks received  
  - `Conversions` – Number of successful actions (e.g., purchases, sign-ups)  
  - `Cost` – Total spend on the campaign  
  - `Revenue` – Revenue generated from the campaign  

> The dataset contains 50 sample rows created for portfolio purposes.

---

## **Key Skills & Concepts Demonstrated**
- **Formulas:** Conversion Rate (`=Conversions/Clicks`), ROI (`=(Revenue-Cost)/Cost`)  
- **PivotTables:** Aggregate metrics by channel or campaign  
- **PivotCharts:** Bar chart for top-performing campaigns, Funnel chart for conversion flow  
- **Conditional Formatting:** Highlight high/low ROI, conversion rates, or campaign performance  
- **KPI Cards:** Total Impressions, Total Clicks, Total Conversions, Overall ROI  
- **Slicers & Filters:** Interactive filtering by campaign or channel  

---

## **Dashboard Features**
1. **Campaign Performance by Channel:** Bar chart showing impressions, clicks, and conversions  
2. **Top 5 Campaigns by ROI:** Column chart highlighting the most profitable campaigns  
3. **Conversion Funnel:** Funnel or stacked chart showing drop-off from impressions → clicks → conversions  
4. **KPI Cards:** Total Impressions, Total Clicks, Total Conversions, Overall ROI  
5. **Conditional Formatting:** Highlight campaigns with high/low ROI or conversion rate  
6. **Interactive Slicers:** Filter dashboard by Channel or Campaign  

---

## **How to Use**
1. Download `Marketing_Campaign_Dashboard.xlsx`.  
2. Open in **Microsoft Excel (2016 or later)**.  
3. Use slicers to filter campaigns or channels dynamically.  
4. Explore PivotTables, charts, and KPI cards for insights.
